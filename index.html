The product

A black box recorder for multi-step agents.

01 / Capture

Record the whole trajectory

Preserve state, actions, tool calls and resulting outcomes in chronological order—not just isolated prompts.

02 / Diagnose

Find the failure path

Reconstruct where an agent looped, used stale context, called the wrong tool or crossed an approval boundary.

03 / Improve

Turn runs into evidence

Compare trajectories and connect agent behaviour to operational, customer and commercial outcomes.

Starting with a small instrumentation and reliability pilot.

AFR Labs is currently speaking with teams running agents that take real actions across customer support, finance, commerce and operations. The first product is a lightweight SDK and failure-path audit; automated anomaly detection and simulation come later.
